Musebot Agents by Oscar Puerto

A jazz band of autonomous agents. They agree who plays the intro, play the tune together, and trade solos, handing the lead from one musician to the next. Each musician is really four agents: a musician, a composer, a synchronizer and an ensemble assistant.

This is a working demonstration of research on Artificial Musical Intelligence carried out at Reykjavik University. In the Musebot Ensemble platform, musical agents can only talk to a central conductor. Here they also talk to each other, negotiating and coordinating the way the musicians of a jazz quartet do, using standard agent protocols (FIPA Contract Net, Request and Query) on the JADE agent platform. The chord chart and the captions below show those conversations as they happen.

Paper Puerto, O. and Thue, D. (2017). A Model of Inter-musician Communication for Artificial Musical Intelligence. International Conference on Computational Creativity (ICCC 2017).
