Summary

I'm a senior frontend developer in Aarhus. I turn business requirements into solutions people can use, lead projects from idea to production, and enjoy helping newcomers grow their skills. My day-to-day is Vue, React and React Native, with the frameworks that go with them: Nuxt, Next.js and Expo.

My background is in Computer Science, with a master's degree specialised in Artificial Intelligence from Reykjavik University. My master's research led to a paper on how artificial musicians can communicate with each other, and this site is a working demonstration of it.

At Salling Group

Salling Group runs some of Denmark's best-known retail brands. Since 2020 I have worked on the customer-facing e-commerce frontends of Bilka (bilka.dk), føtex (foetex.dk) and BR (br.dk). My role has grown steadily, from building features, to running complex ones, to leading people, and now to leading projects while staying hands-on.

  1. 2020

    Frontend Developer

    Joined to help build the e-commerce frontends of Bilka, føtex and BR: the shops where customers browse, compare and buy.

  2. 2021

    Frontend Advanced Developer

    Promoted after a year. I took on more responsibility: running complex features from start to finish, and becoming a key player in the continuous improvement of the e-commerce platforms.

What drives me there

  • Shaping requirements into solutions. I like the moment a business need becomes something concrete a team can build and customers can use.
  • Leading projects. Planning the work, keeping operation running smoothly and being the person the team can rely on.
  • Mentoring newcomers. Helping people who are new to the team and the stack develop their skills.
  • Continuous improvement. A large part of my work is the ongoing improvement of the e-commerce platforms, one release at a time.

Research and publications

Paper · ICCC 2017 · Association for Computational Creativity

A Model of Inter-musician Communication for Artificial Musical Intelligence

Puerto, O. and Thue, D. (2017). International Conference on Computational Creativity.

My master's research (2016 to 2017). In an existing platform, musical software agents could only talk to a central conductor. The paper proposes a model where they also talk to each other, negotiating and coordinating the way the musicians of a jazz quartet do, with standard agent protocols.
